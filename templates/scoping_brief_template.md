*From Analyst to Senior Analyst, Chapter 3. One page. Send it to the stakeholder and ask for a reply by a date.*

**Project:**
**Prepared by:**            **Date:**

| --- | --- |

## Worked example (Paperbark Mutual, fictional)

| Section | Priya's brief |
| --- | --- |
| Question | Is the $100 loyalty credit keeping members who would otherwise leave, or mostly going to members who would have stayed anyway? |
| Decision it supports | Whether to keep, narrow or stop the credit at the January budget review |
| Decision maker and date | Sam Whitfield, with Helen Marsh; budget review on 20 January |
| In scope | Members offered the credit between January and June 2025; renewal outcomes to September 2025; comparison with similar members not offered it |
| Out of scope | Other retention campaigns; a new targeting model (possible follow-up); member survey data |
| Output | A four-page summary with a recommendation, plus the underlying table for Finance |
| Data and dependencies | Campaign list from Marketing (Lena Ortiz); renewal data from the warehouse; Finance to confirm margin per member |
| Risks | Offer wasn't randomised, so the comparison group may differ; campaign list may be incomplete for February |
| Effort and dates | 30 to 40 hours; first findings 5 December; final 12 December |
| Check-ins | 15 minutes with Sam on 21 November and 5 December |
