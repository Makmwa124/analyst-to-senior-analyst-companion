# 90-day plan

*From Analyst to Senior Analyst, Appendix B. About two hours a week on top of your normal work.*

**Start date:**            **Review with manager on:**

## Days 1 to 30: understand

- [ ] Ask your manager the three questions from Chapter 1 and agree the target in writing
- [ ] Get the senior analyst role description; highlight every non-technical line
- [ ] Start a request log (or analyse the one you have)
- [ ] Map your stakeholders: influence, interest and stance
- [ ] Book a conversation with every high-influence stakeholder whose stance is Unknown or Sceptical
- [ ] Start your evidence log

## Days 31 to 60: change how you take work

- [ ] Use the five questions from Chapter 2 on every new request
- [ ] Write a one-page scoping brief for your next piece of work over two days
- [ ] Compare estimates with actual hours; start quoting ranges
- [ ] Set up a regular check-in with your one or two most important stakeholders
- [ ] Use one of the four ways to say no at least once
- [ ] Audit one recurring report or dashboard for use

## Days 61 to 90: widen your reach

- [ ] Follow up on work delivered a month or more ago, and report the result
- [ ] Lead with the answer in every summary, email and slide title
- [ ] Offer regular time to a less experienced colleague
- [ ] Write down one piece of team knowledge (a data trap, a how-to, a definition)
- [ ] Pick one change outside your team and write a one-page proposal
- [ ] Review your evidence log with your manager; identify gaps for next quarter
